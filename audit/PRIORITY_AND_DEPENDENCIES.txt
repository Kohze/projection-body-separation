REVIEW OF THE ARGUMENT

Normalization: Pi K is generated by full facet vectors [0,v_i]; no extra
power of two is present in the maximal-minor volume formula.

Seed volume: the signed rational sum is derived from a simplex truncation
identity with a complete divided-difference proof. Independent positive
triangulations verify the two concrete volumes without relying on that sum.

Auxiliary invariant: lifted minors are computed from actual facet vectors
and supports, not inferred from horizontal minors or an assumed symmetry.
The coordinate-exchange symmetry is not central symmetry of the body.

Recursive growth: the producer uses factorial g-ratios; the checker uses
central binomial coefficients. Generic constant bounds prove root limits,
while the seed-specific inequalities 3<D_j<7 prove strict increase and
give finite rational enclosures for the infinite tail.

Class upper: the mixed potential, its real-state coverage, and its analytic
dimension tails are imported with explicit credit. The conversion to the
finite bound (1051/1000)(n+1)(7137/2500)^n is checked by rational Taylor intervals.

All dimensions: the beta-limit sequence uses growing blocks with residues
o(n). The n>=15500 sequence instead uses the fixed dimension-197 block with
all 197 exact rational residue bounds. These are two distinct explicit recipes.

Testing: corrupt vertices, duplicate vertices, missing simplices, reversed
orientations and invalid probes are rejected. Wrong geometric R or a and
an insufficient comparison block are rejected. The growth checker separately
rejects four altered certificates. Tests exercise proof obligations rather
than asserting floating-point output agreement.

Verification is independent in the algorithmic and exact-arithmetic sense;
all code was developed in the same research session. External human review
and proof-assistant formalization are outstanding. The analytic arguments,
including those in imported preprints, remain subject to mathematical review.
